Restoring Bone After Trauma or Infection

Bone reconstruction surgery is most often needed by patients with post-traumatic injuries, deformities or infections affecting bone structure. It helps restore deformed or damaged bone toward its natural shape and function.

What Is Bone Reconstruction?

Bone reconstruction treats bone deformities caused by trauma, infection or congenital conditions, using either the patient's own bone or donor bone grafts to restore structure and stability.

How Bone Reconstruction Is Performed

  1. The extent of bone damage is assessed via imaging

  2. An autograft (patient's own bone) or allograft (donor bone) is selected

  3. The damaged area is carefully rebuilt and stabilised

  4. Plates, rods or screws are used for severe injuries as needed
