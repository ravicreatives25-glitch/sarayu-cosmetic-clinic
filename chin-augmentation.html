What Is Chin Augmentation?

Chin augmentation reshapes or adds definition to the chin to bring it into better balance with the rest of the face, using either a sliding bone technique or a chin implant depending on the case.

How Chin Augmentation Is Performed

  1. The surgeon evaluates facial balance between the nose, chin and jaw

  2. A small incision is placed below the chin or inside the mouth

  3. The chin bone is reshaped, or an implant is precisely positioned

  4. Symmetry is checked before closing the incision with dissolvable sutures

Frequently Asked Questions

Are chin augmentation results permanent?

Yes — chin implants are designed to be a permanent, lifelong solution.

What are chin implants made of?

Common materials include medical-grade silicone, Gore-Tex, or artificial bone.

Can it be combined with rhinoplasty?

Yes — chin augmentation is often combined with rhinoplasty for more balanced facial results.

What is the recovery time?

Most patients resume daily activities within 1-2 weeks.

Will there be visible stitches?

No — the technique used is minimally invasive with well-concealed incisions.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on the surgical technique chosen — confirmed during consultation.
