What Is Rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty reshapes the bone, cartilage and skin of the nose, changing its size, shape or proportion. It can correct an injury, a congenital feature, or a breathing difficulty, alongside purely cosmetic goals.

How Rhinoplasty Is Performed

  1. The nose is evaluated for both shape and function during consultation

  2. Surgery typically takes two to three hours under anaesthesia

  3. Bone and cartilage are reshaped through an internal or small external incision

  4. Skin is carefully re-draped over the new structure

  5. A splint supports the new shape while initial swelling settles

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recovery time after rhinoplasty?

Full recovery generally takes about two to three weeks.

What does rhinoplasty cost?

Cost depends on the specific nasal deformity being corrected and the treatment approach chosen — book a consultation for an accurate quote.

Are there alternatives to rhinoplasty?

For purely cosmetic reshaping there's no real substitute for surgery, but nasal strips can offer a non-surgical way to ease breathing difficulty without changing the nose's structure.

Will there be a visible scar?

It depends on the extent of correction needed. Open rhinoplasty involves a small incision under the nose that fades significantly within 9-12 months; many cases need no external incision at all.
