What Is Facelift?

A facelift (rhytidectomy) repositions sagging facial tissue and removes excess skin to restore a firmer, more youthful contour — commonly addressing jowls, deep creases and loss of skin elasticity.

How Facelift Is Performed

  1. An incision is placed discreetly near the hairline

  2. Skin is separated from underlying muscle and tissue

  3. Deeper facial tissue is repositioned and jowls are addressed

  4. Underlying muscle is tightened with sutures where needed

  5. Excess skin is trimmed and incisions closed with dissolvable sutures

Frequently Asked Questions

Who typically needs a facelift?

Patients with prominent facial wrinkles, sagging cheek or eye skin, or a drooping jawline are the most common candidates.

Is a facelift only for women?

No — facelift surgery is performed for both men and women seeking a refreshed, natural facial contour.

What's the ideal age for a facelift?

There's no fixed age, as it depends on individual skin and ageing patterns, but most candidates are over 40.

How should I sleep after surgery?

Sleeping on your back with your head elevated is recommended for 2-4 weeks after a facelift.

What does a facelift cost?

Cost varies with the treatment area, amount of skin and fat addressed, and overall case complexity — this is confirmed during consultation.

Does a facelift leave permanent scars?

No — the minimally invasive technique used leaves scars that fade and become far less noticeable as they heal.
