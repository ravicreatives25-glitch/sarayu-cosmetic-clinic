What Is Multiple Lipomas?

Some patients develop multiple lipomas — soft, slow-growing fatty lumps — across different parts of the body rather than a single isolated one. Treatment is planned around each lipoma's size, number and location.

How Multiple Lipomas Is Performed

  1. Each lipoma is assessed for size, depth and location

  2. Smaller lipomas are typically removed via targeted liposuction

  3. Larger lipomas may require a small open excision

  4. Incisions are closed with dissolvable stitches

Frequently Asked Questions

Does treatment require much recovery time?

The minimally invasive approach means recovery is generally quick — around 1-2 weeks.

Do lipomas come back after removal?

Results are generally permanent, and this minimally invasive approach reduces the chance of regrowth.

What does treatment cost?

Cost depends on the number and size of lipomas treated, and the technique used — confirmed during consultation.

Can untreated lipomas become cancerous?

Rarely, but large or painful lipomas left untreated can occasionally develop into a cancerous form, which is one reason timely evaluation matters.

When should I contact the clinic after surgery?

Reach out promptly if you notice fever or chills, redness, swelling, discharge, nausea, vomiting, or an itchy rash.
