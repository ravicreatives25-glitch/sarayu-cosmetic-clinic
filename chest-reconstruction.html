What Is Chest Reconstruction?

Chest reconstruction removes excess tissue and reshapes the chest wall — often called 'top surgery' — to achieve a flatter, more masculine chest contour.

How Chest Reconstruction Is Performed

  1. The chest area is assessed for tissue volume and skin laxity

  2. Small incisions are placed to minimise visible scarring

  3. Excess tissue is removed and the chest wall reshaped

  4. Skin is tightened for a smooth final contour
