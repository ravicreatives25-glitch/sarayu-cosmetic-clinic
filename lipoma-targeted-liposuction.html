Removing Lipomas Without Open Surgery

Lipomas are benign, soft fatty lumps that grow under the skin. While harmless, they can be uncomfortable or cosmetically bothersome. Lipoma targeted liposuction removes them through a fine cannula rather than a traditional open incision.

This technique is best suited to smaller or moderately sized lipomas, and typically leaves a far smaller scar than conventional excision, with a quicker return to normal activity.

What Is Lipoma Targeted Liposuction?

A lipoma is a soft, benign lump of fat that grows beneath the skin, often on the arms, shoulders, neck or thighs. Lipoma targeted liposuction removes it through a fine cannula rather than a larger open incision.

How Lipoma Targeted Liposuction Is Performed

  1. The lipoma and surrounding area are assessed and marked

  2. A small incision allows insertion of a fine suction cannula

  3. Laser-assisted technique may be used to liquefy the fatty tissue

  4. The lipoma is gently suctioned out through the cannula

  5. The small incision is closed with dissolvable stitches

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recovery time?

Recovery generally takes about 8-10 days.

When can I resume daily activities?

Most patients return to their normal routine within 2-3 weeks, avoiding strenuous activity until comfortable.

Are the results permanent?

Yes — removed lipomas do not return in the same spot, giving long-lasting results.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on the size of the lipoma and complexity of removal — confirmed during consultation.

When should I contact the clinic after surgery?

Reach out promptly if you notice fever or chills, redness, swelling, discharge, nausea, vomiting, or an itchy rash.
