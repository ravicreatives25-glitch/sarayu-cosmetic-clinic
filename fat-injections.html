Natural Volume Restoration

Fat injection — also called autologous fat transfer — takes a patient's own fat from one area of the body and re-injects it where volume has been lost, most often in the face. Because it uses the patient's own tissue, the result feels and looks natural.

What Is Fat Injections?

Fat injection (autologous fat transfer) removes fat from one area of the body and re-injects it where volume has been lost — most often the face — using the patient's own tissue for a naturally soft result.

How Fat Injections Is Performed

  1. Fat is gently suctioned from a donor area such as the abdomen or thighs

  2. The collected fat is spun to isolate healthy, viable fat cells

  3. Purified fat is carefully injected into the targeted facial areas

  4. Results are assessed and refined during the same session

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the procedure take?

Typically about one to one and a half hours, depending on the treatment area and number of injections needed.

Are the results permanent?

Yes — fat injection results are generally permanent once the transferred fat has integrated.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on how many injections are needed to achieve the desired result — confirmed during consultation.

Is there post-procedure care needed?

Some swelling and redness is normal; applying cold packs for the first 1-2 days is usually recommended.

Does it require large incisions?

No — fat is transferred entirely through fine injections, with no incisions needed.
