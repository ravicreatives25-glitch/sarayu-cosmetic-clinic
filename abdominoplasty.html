What Is Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck)?

How Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck) Is Performed

  1. Performed under general anaesthesia

  2. Excess sagging skin is marked and removed

  3. Underlying abdominal muscles are brought together and stitched

  4. Remaining skin is pulled taut and any stretch marks trimmed away

  5. Skin is carefully repositioned and incisions closed

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recovery time?

Recovery time varies by patient, but full recovery generally takes about 2-3 weeks.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on the treatment area and technique chosen — confirmed during consultation.

Are results permanent?

Results are long-lasting provided a stable weight and healthy diet are maintained afterward.

How long until skin fully settles?

Tightened skin generally takes about 6 weeks to settle into its final position.

Will my weight change after surgery?

Many patients notice a visible change in shape and sometimes weight following the procedure.
