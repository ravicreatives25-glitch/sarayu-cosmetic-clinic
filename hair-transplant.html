What Is Hair Transplant?

Hair transplantation moves healthy hair follicles from a donor area to thinning or balding areas, where they continue to grow naturally. It suits pattern baldness, traction hair loss, and hair thinning in both men and women.

How Hair Transplant Is Performed

  1. Follicular units are extracted from a healthy donor area (FUE or FUT technique)

  2. Tiny recipient sites are created in the treatment area

  3. Extracted grafts are carefully transplanted to match natural growth direction

  4. The scalp is protected with dressing during initial healing

  5. PRP therapy may be added to further support hair growth

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the surgery take?

Typically 3-4 hours, depending on the extent of hair loss and treatment area.

What is the ideal age for a hair transplant?

Patients aged roughly 20-40 with stabilised hair loss tend to be the most suitable candidates.

What is the recovery time?

Most patients are back to normal within a few days. It's also normal for transplanted hair to shed initially before healthy regrowth begins.

Are there side effects?

The procedure is generally well tolerated with minimal side effects when performed correctly.

Are the results permanent?

Yes — transplanted hair follicles typically provide permanent, long-term results.

What does a hair transplant cost?

Cost depends on the size of the treatment area and the extraction method chosen — confirmed during consultation.
