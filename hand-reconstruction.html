What Is Hand Reconstruction?

Hand reconstruction rebuilds function in hands severely affected by trauma, arthritis or congenital conditions, addressing everything from skin and tendons to joints and nerves.

How Hand Reconstruction Is Performed

  1. The extent of hand damage is carefully assessed

  2. A minimally invasive technique is chosen — graft, flap, fixation, tendon or nerve repair

  3. Damaged structures are repaired or reconstructed

  4. Rehabilitation supports the return of hand function
