Repairing Nerve Injuries in the Limbs

Nerve injuries in the upper or lower limbs commonly occur due to trauma or traction injuries, and can affect sensation and movement. Nerve reconstruction surgery aims to repair this damage and restore function.

What Is Nerve Reconstruction?

Nerve reconstruction repairs nerve injuries in the upper or lower limbs, most often caused by trauma or traction, to restore sensation, movement and normal blood flow.

How Nerve Reconstruction Is Performed

  1. The injured nerve is carefully located and assessed

  2. Where possible, nerve endings are directly reattached

  3. A nerve graft is used if the gap is too large to close directly

  4. Recovery of sensation and movement is monitored over time
